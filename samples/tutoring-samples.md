# Tutoring Portfolio Samples

## Spoken English: Introducing Yourself

**Learning goal:** Learners practise a short, natural self-introduction.

1. Model: “Hello, my name is ____. I’m interested in ____. It’s nice to meet you.”
2. Practise: Say the introduction slowly, then repeat it at a natural pace.
3. Conversation: Ask and answer “What is your name?”, “What are you interested in?”, and “What do you enjoy learning?”
4. Review: Give one encouraging note and one practical suggestion for clearer speech.

## IT: Understanding Files and Folders

**Learning goal:** Learners understand how to organize files on a computer.

1. Explain: A file stores information; a folder helps organize files.
2. Demonstrate: Create a folder named `Practice`, then create descriptive filenames for two example documents.
3. Practise: Ask the learner to create a folder for schoolwork and place sample documents inside it.
4. Review: Ask why clear filenames and folders make information easier to find.
