# Scriptwriting Sample: Small Steps, Lasting Progress

**Format:** Short inspirational video narration  
**Status:** Original portfolio sample  
**Intended audience:** General online audience

## Opening

Big changes rarely begin with one giant leap. More often, they begin with one small decision—the decision to try, to learn, and to keep going.

## Main message

You do not have to understand every step before you begin. Choose one useful thing you can do today. Practise it. Learn from what goes well, and learn from what doesn't. Progress is not always loud or easy to see, but steady effort can take you further than waiting for the perfect moment.

## Closing

Start where you are. Use what you have. Take the next step—and give yourself the chance to grow.

**Production note:** Pair the narration with simple shots of someone learning, practising, and completing a small task. Use calm pacing and readable on-screen captions.
